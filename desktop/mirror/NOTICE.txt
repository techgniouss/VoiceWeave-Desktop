VoiceWeave - copies of third-party speech model files

These files are exact, unmodified copies of files published elsewhere. VoiceWeave downloads them from here first and from the
original address if this copy is unavailable. Each file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 before it is used.

Telugu and Tamil (indicconformer-te-*, indicconformer-ta-*)
  Modified versions of the AI4Bharat IndicConformer models (indicconformer_stt_<lang>_hybrid_rnnt_large), prepared for sherpa-onnx
  by the saeedtechshop/transly-speech-packs project (CTC decoder only, ONNX export, int8 MatMul weights).
  Original: https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformerASR
  Prepared files: https://github.com/saeedtechshop/transly-speech-packs/releases/tag/indic-v1

Hindi (indicconformer-hi-*)
  ONNX export of ai4bharat/indicconformer_stt_hi_hybrid_ctc_rnnt_large by the OpenVoiceOS community.
  https://huggingface.co/OpenVoiceOS/ai4bharat-indicconformer-hi-onnx (commit 8960b8611af5b8c375d442d52907360176410c8b)

Copies of the Telugu, Tamil and Hindi speech files that [VoiceWeave](https://github.com/techgniouss/VoiceWeave-Desktop) downloads when you pick one of those languages.

They are exact copies of files published elsewhere, based on the MIT-licensed [AI4Bharat IndicConformer](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformerASR) models. Each one was checked against a pinned size and SHA-256 before it was published (see `SHA256SUMS`). The sources and the licence text are in `NOTICE.txt`.

You do not need to download anything here yourself: the app does it.

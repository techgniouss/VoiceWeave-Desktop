Models downloaded on first launch (not part of the installer)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 (NVIDIA)             CC-BY-4.0
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2
    ONNX int8 export for sherpa-onnx by csukuangfj:
    https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2-int8
    Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
    The files are downloaded unchanged and used for speech recognition.

Spokenly Cleanup 1 by Spokenly            Apache-2.0
    https://huggingface.co/spokenly/cleanup-1-gguf
    A fine-tune of Qwen3-0.6B-Base (Copyright 2024 Alibaba Cloud, Apache-2.0),
    converted to GGUF with 8-bit weights (Q8_0) by Spokenly.

Wake word model (sherpa-onnx keyword spotter)   Apache-2.0
    sherpa-onnx-kws-zipformer-gigaspeech-3.3M-2024-01-01 by the k2-fsa / icefall authors:
    https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/kws-models
    Downloaded only when the wake word is turned on. About 5 MB of the 17 MB archive is kept, unchanged.

Whisper-base (OpenAI)                     MIT
    https://github.com/openai/whisper
    ONNX int8 export for sherpa-onnx by csukuangfj:
    https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-whisper-base
    Used only to identify the spoken language in the "English + ..." modes.

AI4Bharat IndicConformer (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil)   MIT
    Copyright (c) 2024 AI4Bharat. https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformerASR
    Prepared for sherpa-onnx by others (Telugu and Tamil: https://github.com/saeedtechshop/transly-speech-packs,
    modified: one language's decoder, ONNX, int8; Hindi: https://huggingface.co/OpenVoiceOS/ai4bharat-indicconformer-hi-onnx).
    Exact copies are also kept at https://github.com/techgniouss/VoiceWeave-Desktop/releases/tag/mirror-indic-v1,
    with the licence text, and are tried first; the original address is the fallback.

Voicebox (jamiepine/voicebox)   MIT
    https://github.com/jamiepine/voicebox
    The idea of dropping speech-recogniser repetition loops before cleanup. No code is copied.

Each model is verified against a pinned SHA-256 hash before it is used.

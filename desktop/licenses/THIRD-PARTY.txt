Models downloaded on first launch (not part of the installer)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 (NVIDIA)             CC-BY-4.0
    https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2
    ONNX int8 export for sherpa-onnx by csukuangfj:
    https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-nemo-parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2-int8
    Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
    The files are downloaded unchanged and used for speech recognition.

Spokenly Cleanup 1 by Spokenly            Apache-2.0
    https://huggingface.co/spokenly/cleanup-1-gguf
    A fine-tune of Qwen3-0.6B-Base (Copyright 2024 Alibaba Cloud, Apache-2.0),
    converted to GGUF with 8-bit weights (Q8_0) by Spokenly.

Whisper-base (OpenAI)                     MIT
    https://github.com/openai/whisper
    ONNX int8 export for sherpa-onnx by csukuangfj:
    https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-whisper-base
    Used only to identify the spoken language in the "English + ..." modes.

AI4Bharat IndicConformer (Telugu, Hindi, Tamil)   MIT
    https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformer
    Packs from https://github.com/saeedtechshop/transly-speech-packs and
    https://huggingface.co/OpenVoiceOS/ai4bharat-indicconformer-hi-onnx

Each model is verified against a pinned SHA-256 hash before it is used.
